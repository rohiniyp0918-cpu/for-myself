A letter for you

To the Boy Who Became One of My Favourite Parts of Life ❤️

Dear You,

I don't know if I have ever told you this properly, but I'm genuinely glad that life introduced me to you.

It's funny how some people enter our lives without any big announcement, without any promises, and without us even realising how important they are going to become. And then, one day, we find ourselves smiling at a random message from them, waiting to tell them every little thing that happens, and missing their presence even when we don't want to admit it.

That's you for me.

You're not just someone I talk to when I'm bored. You're someone I want to tell things to, whether it's something exciting, something stupid, or something that doesn't even make sense. Sometimes I don't even need advice; I just need you to listen and be there.

But behind all those , there is something I genuinely want you to know.

You matter to me. I don't know when you became such an important part of my everyday thoughts, but somewhere along the way, you did..

I may not always ask if you're okay, but I hope you know that I care. I hope you never feel that you have to hide your sadness behind a smile when you're talking to me. You don't have to be strong all the time. You can be yourself, even on your worst days.

And now, let me say something, There are so many things I wish I could explain, but I guess not every feeling needs to be spoken out loud. Some things are beautiful even when they remain unspoken..

I don't know what kind of magic you carry, but sometimes even your smallest messages manage to put a smile on my face. Your smile? A little too dangerous. Your voice? Sometimes unnecessarily attractive. And your attitude? Well, let's not discuss that. You already have enough confidence! 😂❤️

If I ever annoy you, it's probably because you're one of my favourite people to annoy. If I ever act childish with you, it's because I feel comfortable enough to be that version of myself.

I don't know what tomorrow looks like. Maybe life will get busy, our priorities will change, and we'll have fewer conversations than we do today. But I sincerely hope we never become those people who once knew everything about each other and eventually become strangers.

You know, if someone asked me what I wish for you, my answer would be simple. I wish you happiness that never feels temporary, success that makes you proud of yourself, and people who understand you even when you don't know how to explain what's going on in your mind.

And selfishly, I hope I get to remain one of those people.

I know life doesn't always happen the way we imagine it. Sometimes, even when a person means so much to us, circumstances have their own plans. Not every beautiful connection gets the ending we secretly wish for. And perhaps that's something we have to accept with a smile, even when our hearts have a different story.

But you know what? I don't want to lose the beautiful bond we have just because life might not give us every possibility.

I'd rather have a place in your life where I can laugh with you, annoy you, celebrate your achievements, listen to your endless stories and remind you that you're capable of so much.

I don't need to be the reason behind every smile of yours. I'm happy if I can be one of the reasons sometimes.🤗

I don't know where life will take us, and I don't want to make promises about things neither of us can control.

All I know is that meeting you is something I would never want to erase from my story.

I cannot promise that I'll always have the perfect words, but I can promise that I'll never fake the care I have for you.

And one more thing...

Maybe I won't get to be a part of every chapter of your life, but I genuinely hope I get to be a character you never have to forget.😁🫠

Thank you for being one of those rare people who make ordinary days feel a little less ordinary.

You're special. Not because you're perfect, but because you're YOU.

With all the affection, endless teasing, a little flirting and lots of care with love,

Your favourite patience tester. ❤️
